# AI Cofounder — Vision

## The Shift

We're living through a fundamental inversion. **The "how" of building is becoming the easy part.** Vibe-coding tools, AI agents, and low-code platforms have democratized the ability to ship software. A solo builder can now stand up in a weekend what used to take a team months.

But this creates a new problem: **people are sprinting to build before knowing _what_ to build.**

They invest weekends and hundreds of dollars into vibe-coding a product — only to realize they still need to understand deployment, distribution, monetization, and market fit. The chase behind the idea was flawed because no one pressure-tested the _idea itself_.

**In a world where building is cheap, judgment becomes the scarce resource.**

## What Is AI Cofounder?

**AI Cofounder is the thought partner you need _before_ you write the first line of code.**

It's the brains and legs that help you brainstorm, critique your initial chain of thoughts, research your market, iterate on your vision, and pressure-test your idea — before you commit to building it.

Think of it as your always-available cofounder who:
- **Won't sugarcoat** — tells you what's actually hard about your idea
- **Does the homework** — researches market size, competition, GTM, and more
- **Scores viability** — gives you a weighted, data-backed assessment unique to your idea
- **Recommends pivots** — suggests iterations that improve viability (niche down, sharpen ICP, tweak the offering)
- **Gets you build-ready** — when you're confident, outputs the `.md` spec files and the first prompt for your vibe-coding tool

Yes, building a side-hustle or a full-time business will be hard. But AI Cofounder helps you navigate the hardest (yet most necessary) decisions — timely.

## For Whom

### Primary: The Millennial Tech Builder

Our V1 user is a **millennial tech employee** — smart, curious, eager, and looking to spend weekends building something real and quick.

They are:
- **Employed full-time** in tech (SWE, PM, designer, data) but building on the side
- **Technically capable** — comfortable with vibe-coding tools (Cursor, Replit, Claude, etc.)
- **Idea-rich, validation-poor** — have 3-5 ideas bouncing around but no structured way to evaluate them
- **Time-constrained** — can't afford to waste a month on an idea that was DOA
- **Pragmatic** — want a real assessment, not hype; want to know "should I actually build this?"

### What They're NOT Looking For
- A generic AI chatbot that says "great idea!"
- A 50-page business plan generator
- An MBA-style framework with no actionable output

### What They ARE Looking For
- A fast, honest evaluation of whether their idea has legs
- Specific, weighted criteria that matter for _their_ specific idea
- Actionable iterations that improve viability
- A bridge from "validated idea" to "let's build" — the spec files and prompts to start vibe-coding

## Core Beliefs

1. **Ideas are dime-a-dozen. Prompt engineering and vibe-coding are not.** — The investment to build is real (time + money). The investment to _think_ should come first.
2. **Viability is multi-dimensional and context-dependent.** — "Market size" matters differently for a B2B SaaS vs. a consumer app vs. a hardware product. Scoring must be dynamic.
3. **Iteration beats perfection.** — The goal isn't one perfect idea. It's rapid cycles of refine → score → refine until the builder feels confident.
4. **The output should be actionable.** — The end state isn't a report. It's a set of `.md` files and a prompt that lets you open your vibe-coding tool and start building.
5. **Not every idea needs to be a unicorn.** — Some ideas are for fun, for learning, for a niche audience. AI Cofounder respects that spectrum — from weekend project to serious venture.

## The Product in One Sentence

> **Share your 2-line vision. Get a viability score. Iterate with your AI cofounder. Walk away with the specs to start building.**
